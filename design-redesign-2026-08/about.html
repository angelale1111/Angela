About Dr. Angela Le

Dr. Le is a Doctor of Acupuncture and Chinese Medicine, in practice since 2001, and one of the country’s early integrative fertility acupuncturists. Over decades of clinical practice, she has worked with thousands of women through health challenges and major reproductive life transitions.

Her work has continued to evolve alongside her own life, moving beyond medicine toward deeper questions of how we live, how we love, and what becomes possible when we no longer live from survival.

Dr. Le is the founder of Fifth Avenue Fertility Wellness in New York City. Her private mentorship brings together work that has been developing over many years, shaped by both her life and career.

The Evolution of the Work

My foundation was Traditional Chinese Medicine and Taoist study. At Yo San University, I trained with Master Ni and his sons, heirs to a seventy-four-generation Taoist lineage, studying medicine alongside Taoist philosophy, meditation, Qi Gong, Tai Chi, and sexual practices.

Integrative health and longevity science later added another lens through which to understand physiology, resilience, and vitality.

Somatic Experiencing, Polyvagal Theory, and Internal Family Systems deepened my understanding of the nervous system, including how protective responses can continue long after the original experience has passed.

Somatic sex education expanded the work into intimacy, embodiment, pleasure, and our relationship with our own bodies. I became certified as a Somatic Sexuality Healing Practitioner (SSHP), deepening my work in these areas.

Nondual inquiry brought me into questions that cannot always be solved through effort, including questions of identity, the nature of self, and what remains when we look beyond the stories we have come to believe about ourselves.

Each discipline has shaped me.

None of them, on its own, defines the work.

What matters is the ability to draw from what is useful, without forcing a person into a particular framework.
