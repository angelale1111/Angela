Private Mentorship

A six- to twelve-month engagement for women who are used to finding their own answers and have reached a point where another perspective may be useful.

There is no standardized curriculum or fixed sequence. The work develops in response to what is present, with enough time and continuity to notice what changes, what repeats, and what may need deeper attention.

How It Works

  • Two to three private sessions each month
  • Virtual or in person in New York City
  • Support between sessions
  • Personalized practices and resources when appropriate
  • Priority scheduling
  • A six- to twelve-month commitment

This work asks for openness, honesty, and a willingness to remain curious about what is present.

Going Deeper

At certain points, more concentrated time can be valuable.

Mentorship may include a private day-long or weekend immersion when there is something that would benefit from more sustained attention.

For an individual, that time allows us to stay with a particular issue or experience more fully than we can in a standard session.

For couples, an immersion may focus on communication, emotional safety, intimacy, recurring conflict, or reconnecting with one another.

Each immersion is designed individually and introduced when it feels relevant to the work.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from therapy?

Many of the women I work with also have a therapist, and the two can complement one another.

Private mentorship is not psychotherapy. Our work centers on what is happening in your life now, including the patterns, choices, relationships, and questions that are emerging in real time.

When therapy is part of your support system, this work can exist alongside it.

How is mentorship different from clinical care?

Private mentorship is separate from my clinical practice at Fifth Avenue Fertility Wellness.

Clinical care is focused on health and treatment within the scope of my acupuncture and Chinese medicine practice. Mentorship is a sustained, individualized relationship that can move across the broader areas of your life.

If you are looking for clinical care, you can visit Fifth Avenue Fertility Wellness.

Why is the mentorship six to twelve months?

Meaningful patterns rarely change all at once.

Working together over time allows us to notice what repeats, stay with what changes, and give new ways of responding the opportunity to become more deeply integrated.

The length of the engagement depends on the nature of the work and what unfolds along the way.

Are sessions in person or virtual?

Both.

I work with clients virtually and in person in New York City. Most sessions take place virtually.

Do I need to be at a crisis point?

No.

You do not need to be in crisis for something in your life to deserve deeper attention.

And if you are in a period of real upheaval, this work can meet you there too.

Often, the recognition that something needs to change comes long before anything has fallen apart.

Do I need to believe anything?

No.

There is no philosophy, belief system, or particular way of seeing the world that you are expected to adopt.

We begin with your own experience and what is present in your life.

What is the investment?

Private mentorship is a significant investment of time, attention, and financial resources.

Once your application has been reviewed, we will be in touch to schedule a call and walk through all the specifics together.
